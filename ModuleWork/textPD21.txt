Ryan Lott is a composer, producer, and performer. In 2007, Ryan
founded Son Lux, and in 2014, Son Lux became a trio, both live and on
record, with the additions of guitarist-composer Rafiq Bhatia and
drummer lan Chang. Their latest studio release is an album trilogy
called Tomorrows, released in full in 2021. As a band, Son Lux scored
the 2023 Best Picture Winner Everything Everywhere All at Once
(Original Motion Picture Soundtrack), which earned them a BAFTA and
Academy Award nomination, with a second Academy Award
nomination for their end-credit song collaboration with Mitski and
David Byrne.
